Persist until
you succeed.

Why I kept going when the work was tedious, and what persistence has meant for my archive.

From my book in progress, The Guide I Needed · Chapter 3

In this chapter, I drew on ideas from Napoleon Hill’s Think and Grow Rich and Og Mandino’s The Greatest Salesman in the World.

I first heard this idea in Think and Grow Rich, and then in Og Mandino’s The Greatest Salesman in the World: “I WILL PERSIST UNTIL I SUCCEED.” Please hang this sentence across from your bed or somewhere you can see it. I want you to make this sentence part of your identity. The best way to learn is through experience; turn this sentence into experience. The first rule of success comes from failure: if you are going to succeed, you have to fail. When I was making the archive, I couldn’t translate it into English. I wanted to take the easy way and let AI do it, but it ruined the images and mixed up the text. The first drafts of the archive were 266–366 pages long (the current version is a tighter 100 pages), and if I had not put in the effort, I probably wouldn’t have bothered. Persisting is why my archive is about to be added to Columbia University’s collections. It also got me a news story on Turk of America. That was me persisting: even though what I was doing was tedious (actually, what you’re doing usually isn’t hard; it’s tedious, or you haven’t fully understood it yet), I kept going. So don’t stop: “PERSIST UNTIL YOU SUCCEED.”

If you persist, if you keep trying, if you keep pushing forward, you will succeed. I WILL SUCCEED! Persist until you succeed. You did not come into this world to be defeated; neither did I. We are not sheep waiting to be herded by a shepherd. We are lions, and we refuse to walk, talk and sleep with sheep. WE PERSIST UNTIL WE SUCCEED. I will not listen to those who always complain and never try, because their disease is contagious. Let them join the sheep; we will be lions. Remember why you started this journey: Is there a house or a car you want to buy for your mom and dad? Do it, just do it, do what you love.

I will persist until I succeed, we will persist!

In life, rewards are at the end of the journey, not at the beginning. Maybe you do it the first time, but you can’t do it the second time, and you think about giving up. Right at that moment, take a pen and paper and write down why. Stop using silly words like “I can’t,” “impossible,” “never.” You are you; you can do it. Every “no” brings you closer to a “yes.”

Keep Going…

PERSIST UNTIL YOU SUCCEED
