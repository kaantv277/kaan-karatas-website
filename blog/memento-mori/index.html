Live today as if it were your last day: five principles against procrastination.

From my book in progress, Guide to Success · Chapter 5

Live today as if it were your last day. Even if the things you do feel boring, know how valuable they are. You will understand this better at times when you feel stuck, or feel like you are rowing a boat across the ocean. You start sentences with “I wish”: “I wish I had 1 free day, a day when my mind could rest…” That last day, I will live as if it were my last. Don’t let yesterday’s sadness affect your today. Why would you sacrifice today’s good for yesterday’s bad? Don’t wear yourself out with things that will never happen. Don’t imagine winning for the 25th time in that scenario you built with the person who is always on your mind; don’t let them steal your energy. Who are you trying to prove what to? You only prove to yourself that you can do it. Once you do it, more will follow. The only thing you can control is today. When you think about putting something off until tomorrow, remember this: you will probably put it off again tomorrow. Saying “tomorrow” is easy; the brain tries to spend the least energy with the least effort and to save energy. Destroy procrastination with action. If you have a child, go and love them, stroke their hair, give them your love unconditionally. Don’t leave them longing for love, because one day they and I will both be gone. The things you have today are not things you will have forever. Here are 5 principles for this:

Five principles

  • Humility
  • Awareness
  • Meaning
  • Responsibility
  • Action
“Awareness Brings Control”

– Baraneemo

These words mean a lot to me. I first saw them on the Baraneemo channel, in Baran’s podcast. Being reminded that there is only today was, for me, like watching procrastination die. It was an awakening, and I immediately hung it on my bedroom wall so I could see and remember it every day. If you make these words part of your identity, you become unstoppable. Finally:
